# Code of Conduct

Short version: be someone people want to open a PR with again.

## The standard

- Disagree with the idea, not the person. "This selector strategy breaks on
  shadow DOM" is useful. "This is a dumb idea" is not — say what breaks and why.
- Assume good faith on a first message. Most confusing PRs and terse issues are
  someone in a hurry, not someone being difficult.
- No harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in issues, PRs, discussions, or
  anywhere else this project's name is attached.

## Scope

This applies anywhere the project is discussed under its own name — this repo's
issues, PRs, and discussions, and anywhere else you're representing it.

## Reporting

If someone's conduct falls short of this, email `contact@vexralabs.com`. Reports
are handled directly by the maintainer and kept as confidential as the situation
allows.

## Enforcement

Consequences scale with severity and pattern — a private word first, in most
cases. Repeated or severe violations get a public or private ban from the
project's spaces.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), trimmed to what this project actually needs.
